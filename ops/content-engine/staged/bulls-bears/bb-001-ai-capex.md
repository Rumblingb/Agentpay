**$725B AI Capex: Moat Or Bubble?**

Companies are on pace to spend $725B on AI infrastructure this cycle.

The Bull case: this is the new cloud layer — durable, infrastructure-grade demand that compounds for a decade.
The Bear case: the GPU cycle repeats. We spent the same in 2001, got wiped, and rebuilt 5 years later.

Both can be true in sequence. The question is where we are in the sequence.

Pull the capex line and the earnings margins side by side. Capital intensity is rising. Revenue per dollar of infra spend is the metric that separates a moat from a bubble.
