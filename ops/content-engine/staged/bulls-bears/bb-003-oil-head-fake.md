**$100 Oil: Inflation Shock Or Head Fake?**

Oil touched $100. That headline has a discount built in.

Bear oil case: Iran sanctions were loosened, SPR releases were bigger than expected, the demand call from China is softening, and the supply side has maxed out at this ramp rate.

Bull oil case: SPR capacity is finite, geopolitical risk hasn't priced out a disorderly escalation, and refinery runs in the US are still tight heading into summer.

Both are real. The SPX reaction to $100 oil matters more than the oil price itself — equities repricing triggers the self-fulfilling slowdown.
