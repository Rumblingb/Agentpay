## Description
You took office on the worst possible day.

The AI companies now out-earn the entire old economy — and they fund your campaign. The data centers want eleven gigawatts you don't have. An AI agent just filed to vote, and the case is airtight. Your advisors file in one at a time, each with an impossible decision, and only you can make the call.

Swipe left or right. Every choice moves four forces — Treasury, People, Military, Planet. Let any one of them hit zero or boil over and your reign ends, usually badly. Survive as many days as you can, then try to outlast yourself.

• One-thumb play — swipe to decide, that's the whole game
• Sharp, near-future dilemmas about AI, power, money and the planet
• Four hidden meters you're always quietly balancing
• A fresh run every time — the deck reshuffles
• Darkly funny obituaries when it all falls apart
• No account, no ads mid-decision, plays fully offline

You will not last forever. The question is how long, and what you're willing to trade to get there.
